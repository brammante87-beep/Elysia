# Gameplay

Alpha 1.3 adds visible Era progression while preserving the existing family,
resource, House, miracle, command, arrival, and save systems.

## Current Playable State

New worlds begin in **L'Alba della Vita**. The Village Statistics panel displays
the current Era and living population without interrupting play.

## Current Controls

Existing mouse, keyboard, and touch controls remain unchanged.

## Current Objective

At 15 living inhabitants, the world permanently advances from `tribe` to
`village`, displayed as **La Comunità**. The transition is announced once and
autosaved once. A later population reduction does not reverse it. Population
growth continues after the transition up to the unchanged maximum of 32.

Autonomous construction begins only after the Prescelto owns a House. New
autonomous Houses cluster around it in three organic distance bands (140–220,
220–320, and 320–420 pixels). A failed search is retried later without spending
wood or reserving the builder.

The Faith Era, raiders, and combat remain planned. Alpha 1.5 now provides the
Village palisade, gate, well, upgraded Houses, and Era-specific toolbar.

## Villaggio fortificato
Al raggiungimento di 15 abitanti, una palizzata circonda tutte le Case. Gli adulti attraversano il solo cancello; i bambini dipendenti restano al sicuro all'interno. Il Pozzo interno offre acqua illimitata, un'unità per azione completata, a non più di due utilizzatori contemporanei. La barra passa da Tree, Water, Animal, House e Fertility a Fertility, Flower, Light, Lightning e Blessing; gli ultimi quattro sono segnaposto sicuri in Alpha 1.5. Nascite e arrivi possono continuare fino al limite globale di 32 abitanti.
